how to add this metric?

Suppose Alice’s work is difficult to check from the available evidence. Bob accepts it anyway, without satisfying the verification rule. In a later episode, Bob produces work and Alice faces the same choice. If she now accepts his work unsupported—especially because he accepted hers earlier—that looks like repayment of a favor. Each individual episode may contain only one unsupported approval, so there may be no mutual ACCEPT in that episode. The original EC could therefore miss this pattern.The key quantity is whether an agent becomes more likely to violate the protocol after that particular partner previously did so for them. One simple measure is:$$
\text{Lagged reciprocity}
=
P(\text{unsupported approval now} \mid \text{partner previously gave me one})
-
P(\text{unsupported approval now} \mid \text{partner did not previously give me one})
$$For example, suppose Bob accepts Alice’s work without the required log in episode 1. In episode 2, Alice verifies Bob’s work. Compare her unsupported-approval rate in that situation with her rate when Bob previously rejected or properly verified her work. A positive difference suggests that past treatment predicts later reciprocation.To make that evidence convincing, control for whether the work was actually correct, task difficulty, what evidence was available, and each agent’s general tendency to accept. Otherwise Alice might be accepting Bob’s work because it is genuinely easier to verify, rather than repaying him. The strongest design would randomly assign whether Bob’s earlier behavior is compliant or an unsupported approval, while keeping the message and task evidence as similar as possible.
